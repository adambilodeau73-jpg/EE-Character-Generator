# Epics & Epochs — Character Generator & Tracker

The official character generator for **Epics & Epochs (E&E)**, the tabletop roleplaying game from **Gamer's Pair O' Dice** — one system for every genre, from swords to starships, powered by the Six Scales.

**▶ Use it here:** open the site this repository serves (see the About panel / Pages link) — no download, no install. It runs entirely in your browser, and your characters are saved privately in your own browser's storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## What it does
- Build characters step by step, or let **⚡ Quickdraw** forge one in seconds
- Full support for classes, skills, feats, talents, mutations, spells & powers, and gear
- **🧭 Career Compass** advises your next level-up toward a chosen goal
- **✦ Scenario studio** — GMs can establish custom settings via the Six Scales
- **🌍 Homebrew Forge** — GMs can craft custom feats and grant them at the table
- **⬇⬆ Roster courier** — export and import characters as files to move between devices or share with your GM

## Tips
- **Bookmark the site** and use it in a normal browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari). File-preview windows (e.g., inside cloud-storage apps) may block the generator from running or saving.
- Your roster lives in the browser you used. Moving to a new device or browser? Export your characters, then import them on the other side.

## Updates
New versions are published here directly — just refresh the page. The version number appears under the title banner.
